Manuscript Submission

The International Conference on Recent Advances in Engineering Sciences and Applications (RAESA 2027) invites academicians, researchers, industry professionals, and students to submit original, unpublished, and high-quality research papers across engineering, science, mathematics, artificial intelligence, data science, machine learning, statistics, physics, chemistry, and related interdisciplinary domains.

All manuscripts will undergo a rigorous peer-review process and will be evaluated on originality, technical quality, research contribution, relevance to the conference, clarity of presentation, and overall significance. Acceptance will be communicated by email to the corresponding author. Accepted papers must submit the revised camera-ready version within the stipulated deadline.

Sponsorship: Viswagandha Science Society, Durgapur has accepted the sponsorship.

Paper Submission Links

Authors are requested to submit their full papers before the deadline listed on the conference homepage. Manuscripts should align with the conference theme and conform to the official conference paper format.

Author Guidelines

RAESA 2027 invites original, unpublished, and technically sound contributions in engineering, applied sciences, mathematics, statistics, computer science, and related interdisciplinary areas.

  • Only original and unpublished research contributions will be considered.
  • Manuscripts should be prepared in the official conference format and written in English.
  • Submissions should clearly present the problem, novelty, methodology, results, and contribution.
  • Accepted papers must be presented by at least one registered author at the conference.
  • Papers should be submitted through the Microsoft CMT portal before the deadline.
  • All submissions will undergo a rigorous peer-review process.
  • Authors are responsible for ensuring the originality of the work and proper citation of prior research.
  • Accepted manuscripts must follow the final camera-ready requirements before publication.

Publication Workflow

  1. Submit the manuscript via the CMT portal.
  2. Receive peer-review feedback and revision suggestions.
  3. Prepare and submit the revised version after acceptance.
  4. Submit the final camera-ready paper within the announced deadline.
  5. Present the paper during the conference.

Plagiarism Policy

Authors are responsible for ensuring the originality of their submissions. The manuscript should be checked using licensed plagiarism-detection software before submission.

  • All papers must comply with the conference plagiarism and originality guidelines.
  • Any form of plagiarism or self-plagiarism is strictly prohibited.
  • Proper citation must be provided for all previously published work, figures, tables, datasets, or concepts used in the manuscript.
  • Permission must be obtained before using copyrighted material.

Submission Enquiries

For any submission-related queries, please contact the RAESA 2027 Organizing Committee.
