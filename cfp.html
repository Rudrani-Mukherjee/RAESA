Call for Papers

RAESA 2027 spans two days and features paper presentations, keynote lectures, and panel discussions. Researchers, academicians, industry professionals, and students from all backgrounds are warmly invited to take part in this interdisciplinary conference.

All accepted and presented papers will be considered for publication in a Scopus-indexed Springer / Taylor & Francis / Wiley conference proceedings, subject to peer review. Authors are invited to submit original, unpublished manuscripts in PDF format, following the IEEE double-column format and limited to 6 pages (2 additional pages permitted at an extra charge), via the Microsoft CMT submission portal. RAESA 2027 will exclusively review full papers that fall within the conference's scope.

Submission Guidelines

Authors are invited to submit original and unpublished research papers related to the conference themes. Submitted manuscripts must not be under consideration for publication elsewhere.

  • Original research papers only.
  • All papers will undergo peer review.
  • Accepted papers will require camera-ready submission.
  • Paper submission through Microsoft CMT.
  • Accepted papers will be considered for publication in Springer / Taylor & Francis / Wiley Proceedings (Scopus Indexed), subject to peer review.
  • Publication charges, if applicable, shall be borne by the authors.
