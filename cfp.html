Call for Papers

RAESA 2027 is a two-day interdisciplinary conference featuring paper presentations, keynote lectures, and panel discussions. Researchers, academicians, industry professionals, and early-career scientists are cordially invited to submit their original research contributions.

Accepted papers will be published in Scopus-indexed conference proceedings by Springer / Taylor & Francis / Wiley (subject to peer review). Submit original, unpublished manuscripts in IEEE double-column format (maximum 6 pages, with 2 additional pages available at extra charge) via the Microsoft CMT submission portal.

Presentation Modes

  • Oral Presentation
  • Poster Presentation

Evaluation Criteria

Contributions will be evaluated based on scientific relevance, originality, methodological rigor, and potential impact. Abstracts and full-length research papers are welcome across all conference themes.

Submission Guidelines

Authors are invited to submit original and unpublished research papers related to the conference themes. Submitted manuscripts must not be under consideration for publication elsewhere.

  • Original research papers only.
  • All papers will undergo peer review.
  • Accepted papers will require camera-ready submission.
  • Paper submission through Microsoft CMT.
  • Accepted papers will be considered for publication in Springer / Taylor & Francis / Wiley Proceedings (Scopus Indexed), subject to peer review.
  • Publication charges, if applicable, shall be borne by the authors.
