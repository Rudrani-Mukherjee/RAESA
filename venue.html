Established in 2000, Dr. B. C. Roy Engineering College (BCREC), Durgapur, is one of the leading private engineering institutions in West Bengal. Affiliated with MAKAUT, West Bengal, approved by AICTE, and with several programs accredited by NBA, the college is recognized for its commitment to academic excellence, research, and innovation.

Spread across a 17-acre green campus, BCREC offers modern infrastructure, including well-equipped laboratories, smart classrooms, a central library, auditoriums, sports facilities, hostels, and advanced research centers. The institution is supported by experienced faculty members and a dedicated Training and Placement Cell that prepares students for successful careers in academia and industry.

As the host institution of RAESA 2027, Dr. B. C. Roy Engineering College warmly welcomes academicians, researchers, industry professionals, and students from around the world for an enriching exchange of knowledge, innovation, and collaboration.
