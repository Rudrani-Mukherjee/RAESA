Program Schedule

RAESA 2027 will be organized into keynote sessions, invited talks, parallel technical sessions, and poster presentations to ensure effective dissemination of knowledge.

TimeSession

Highlights

Additional Academic Features

4–6 Parallel Technical Tracks

Focused sessions spanning Mathematics & Statistics, Physics, Chemistry, Data Science & AI, and Engineering Applications.

2 Keynote Lectures

Distinguished international and national speakers on emerging trends in interdisciplinary research.

2–4 Invited Talks

Expert lectures from leading national researchers across the conference themes.

Best Paper Award

Outstanding contributions recognized track-wise during the valedictory session.

Best Poster Award

Jury-evaluated poster session for students and early-career researchers.

Young Researcher Recognition

Encouraging student participation and celebrating emerging research talent.
